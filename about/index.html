ABOUT ME

Hi! I am currently a Ph.D. candidate studying human factors at the University of Windsor. My research is about human vigilance performance and cognitive underload when interacting with automation systems. I am interestd in how techonoloy influence human cognitive performance, how to design products or services that suit human needs, and how to create efficient human-machine interactions.
